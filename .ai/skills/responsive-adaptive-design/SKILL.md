# Responsive / Adaptive Design

Adapt the interface; do not amputate it.

Check:
- narrow mobile,
- wider mobile/tablet,
- desktop,
- enlarged text/zoom,
- touch/coarse pointer,
- keyboard-only.

Decide intentionally:
- what stacks,
- what reorders,
- what becomes a drawer,
- what remains sticky,
- what scrolls,
- what condenses,
- what must remain reachable.

Rules:
- preserve primary actions,
- preserve logical reading order,
- avoid hover-only functionality,
- do not hide critical functionality for screenshot cleanliness,
- add breakpoints where content or interaction needs them,
- test long/localized content.

Product UI sidebars, filters, tables and toolbars often need interaction redesign, not width reduction.
