# Product Surface Classification

Before visual design, classify the target surface.

## Modes
### product
Repeated authenticated work. Optimize task completion, clarity, recovery, predictable controls and low cognitive load.

### dashboard-data
Scanning/comparison/action on records and status. Optimize density, tables/lists, filters, state visibility and fast navigation.

### brand-marketing
Public communication/conversion. More expressive typography, composition, imagery and narrative may be justified.

### hybrid
Keep product and marketing regions intentionally separate. Do not leak marketing spectacle into recurring workflows.

## Evidence
Use route purpose, user journey, authentication boundary, frequency of use, content type, and `PRODUCT.md`.

## Guardrail
A third-party design skill or inspiration reference never overrides the chosen surface mode. A stylish pattern that reduces task clarity is a regression on product surfaces.

Record the result in `PRODUCT.md` / `DESIGN.md` when durable.
